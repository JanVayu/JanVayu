What it is

A public record, not a campaign

  • Non-partisan & citizen-led — names problems and sources, endorses no party.
  • Built on public data — CPCB, WAQI, OpenAQ, NASA, Open-Meteo.
  • Open & free forever — anyone can read, reuse or improve it; no ads.
  • Every figure sourced & fact-checked — weekly, against primary sources.

JanVayu turns India’s scattered air-quality data into one record any citizen, journalist, or policymaker can actually use — and keeps it honest.

Accountability

Following the promises & the money

  • NCAP & GRAP trackers — targets, deadlines, stage history.
  • Budget tracker — per-city NCAP spend, CREA-sourced.
  • One-click RTI — pre-filled Right-to-Information templates.
  • City scorecards — who’s acting, who isn’t.

Only 27 of 96 cities with sufficient data met the NCAP 40% PM10 target by the 2026 deadline (CREA). The record is public.

Trust

Every number, fact-checked — weekly

  • Automated weekly audit — every statistic re-verified against primary sources.
  • Public findings — corrections logged in the open repository.
  • Never invent — unverifiable figures are flagged, not guessed.

Clean air is a right — the case for it never needs an exaggerated number. If a figure looks off, tell us; being corrected in public is the point.
