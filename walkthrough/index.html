About this walkthrough

The deck is a one-pass tour of the platform as it stands in May 2026 (v26.6.22). It is built as a teaching reference: every panel is shown with a live screenshot, four short bullets that name the panel's purpose and choices, and spoken-delivery notes that explain why each panel exists and how someone might use it in research, writing, or advocacy. The deck is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 like the rest of JanVayu content; the underlying PowerPoint is available above for adaptation and re-presentation under that licence.

For the most up-to-date version of any panel, click through to the live site rather than relying on the screenshot. Live data, rankings, and budget figures update in real time on janvayu.in; the deck captures the state at the time of recording. Specific dated claims (NCAP deadline elapsed 31 March 2026, CSE Five-Year Review April 2026, IQAir 2025 release, CAQM first off-season GRAP-I on 19 May 2026) are anchored to dated primary sources in the Research Library.
