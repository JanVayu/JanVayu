About this walkthrough

This deck is built natively inside the site — plain HTML, self-hosted fonts, and the same hand-drawn diagrams you see across JanVayu. There is no Google dependency and nothing to re-export, so it stays in step with the platform as we ship changes. Navigate with the arrow keys, press S for speaker notes, O for the slide overview, F for fullscreen, and use your browser's Print for a clean one-slide-per-page PDF.

For live figures — current AQI, rankings, budget spend — click through to the site rather than the slides; those update in real time. The deck is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 like the rest of JanVayu content. Dated claims (the NCAP deadline of 31 March 2026, the IQAir 2025 World Air Quality Report, the Lancet Countdown 2025) are anchored to their primary sources in the Research Library.
