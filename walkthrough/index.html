Walkthrough

A walkthrough of JanVayu — a 64-slide guided tour through the platform built for the MMSF Fellows cohort under the AirQuality for Janhit programme (AIPC). Use the embedded deck below, or download the source files to present offline.

Open in Google Slides (view-only) Download PDF (13 MB) Download PPTX (24 MB · editable)

What this walkthrough covers

  • How JanVayu is structured — the dashboard, the 40+ panels, the role-based landing experience
  • Live AQI data sources (WAQI, CPCB CAAQMS, Sensor.Community) and how they flow through the Netlify Functions tier
  • The accountability layer — NCAP budget tracker, mission tracker, political accountability, RTI templates
  • The seven learning games, including the new Vayu Junction word-grouping puzzle
  • How to fork JanVayu for a new city or country — cost, setup, attribution
