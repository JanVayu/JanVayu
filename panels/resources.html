Analysis CREA · 2026
CREA: How much of India's PM2.5 is secondary? (2026)
Up to 42% of India's PM2.5 is chemically formed in the air, not directly emitted. SO₂ (India is the world's top emitter; coal power = 60%+ of it) is the dominant precursor, forming ammonium sulphate — ~1/3 of Delhi's PM2.5, up to 49% post-monsoon. Yet ~78% of coal plants still lack FGD. MERRA-2, 2024.
