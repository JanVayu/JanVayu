Clean Air Budget Tracker

Following the money: tracking India's air quality investments across NCAP, PMUY, E-DRIVE, and crop residue management schemes. Compiled from Union Budget documents, RTI responses, parliamentary replies, and CREA analysis.

Funding Cliff: 15th Finance Commission grants (₹16,539 Cr for 42 million-plus cities; PIB, Lok Sabha reply, Feb 2024) expired 31 March 2026. As of mid-May 2026, no successor mechanism has been formally announced — cities are operating on residual previously-released allocations. Update, 2 October 2026: the Ministry’s replies of 20 and 23 July 2026 report releases through FY 2025-26 and name no successor grant; we have found no formal announcement. Of the ₹13,415 crore released to NCAP cities up to CREA’s January 2026 cut-off, ₹11,021 crore (82%) came through this grant (CREA 2026). Update, 2 October 2026: PIB (23 July 2026) reports ₹16,423.56 crore released through FY 2025-26, of which ₹13,561.52 crore (83%) via the XV-FC grant. The 16th Finance Commission submitted its report on 17 November 2025 and it was tabled in Parliament on 1 February 2026 (PRS); its award period runs 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031, so the transfer cycle continues without a gap. Update, 5 October 2026: the Commission's report (Volume I, paragraph 10.32) records the Environment Ministry's request to continue the air-quality grant for 42 million-plus cities, add Kannur, Kochi and Kollam, and include 51 cities of 5 to 10 lakh, but the Commission's own local-body award (Table 10.5, Rs 7,91,493 crore for 2026-31) has no air-quality component in the text we extracted. Whether the Union government funds a successor outside the Commission is not confirmed. See Delhi for the detail.

MoEFCC Budget Trend (2019-2026)
Stagnant at 0.07% of Union Budget: Despite air pollution emerging as India's leading environmental health crisis, MoEFCC allocation has stayed between 0.06% and 0.1% of Union spending (PRS). The 2025-26 allocation of ₹3,413 Cr represents less than 0.08% of total Union expenditure (BE 2025-26).

Key Accountability Gaps

Structural Issues

  • No legally binding targets: NCAP targets are not legally binding (Sci. Total Environ. 2025 review)
  • XV-FC cliff: ₹16,539 Cr expired 31 Mar 2026; no dedicated successor to the air-quality grant announced as of mid-May 2026 (still none found as of 2 Oct 2026) — 16th Finance Commission report was submitted 17 Nov 2025 (award period 2026-31)
  • Focus mismatch: 68% on dust vs <1% on industry/domestic fuel
  • Missing studies: 40 of 130 cities lack source apportionment (CREA 2026)
  • Monitoring gaps: 28 cities still without CAAQMS after 7 years (CREA 2026)

Hidden Funds

  • Environmental Cess: ₹45.8 Cr collected over 8 years, <1% spent on pollution control (CPCB RTI reply; The Wire/TOI, Jun 2025)
  • CAMPA Funds: ₹26,001 Cr utilized of ₹29,311 Cr released to State forest departments (₹38,516 Cr approved), 2019-20 to 2023-24 (CEC report, as reported by The Hindu)
Sources: RTI responses, CREA 2026, CEC report (via The Hindu), Parliamentary Questions
