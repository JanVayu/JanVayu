Clean Air Budget Tracker

Following the money: tracking India's air quality investments across NCAP, PMUY, E-DRIVE, and crop residue management schemes. Verified data from Union Budget documents, RTI responses, and CREA analysis.

Funding Cliff: 15th Finance Commission grants (₹16,539 Cr for 42 million-plus cities) expired 31 March 2026. As of mid-May 2026, no successor mechanism has been formally announced — cities are operating on residual previously-released allocations. This bucket represented 87% of all NCAP city funding. The 16th Finance Commission submitted its report on 17 November 2025; its award period runs 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031, so the transfer cycle continues without a gap. What has not been publicly confirmed is whether a dedicated successor to the expired air-quality (million-plus cities) grant will be included. Watch this space.

Key Accountability Gaps

Structural Issues

  • No legally binding targets: NCAP targets are aspirational with no penalties
  • XV-FC cliff: ₹16,539 Cr expired 31 Mar 2026; no dedicated successor to the air-quality grant announced as of mid-May 2026 — 16th Finance Commission report was submitted 17 Nov 2025 (award period 2026-31)
  • Focus mismatch: 68% on dust vs <1% on industry/domestic fuel
  • Missing studies: 40 of 131 cities lack source apportionment
  • Monitoring gaps: 28 cities still without CAAQMS after 7 years

Hidden Funds

  • Environmental Cess: ₹45.8 Cr collected over 8 years, <1% spent on pollution control (RTI)
  • CAMPA Funds: only ₹26,002 Cr utilized (2019-24), a fraction of the accumulated afforestation corpus (CAG 2024)
  • State Boards: Many SPCBs under-resourced despite fee collections
  • Health Budget: No dedicated respiratory health allocation linked to AQ
Sources: RTI responses, CAG Reports, Parliamentary Questions
