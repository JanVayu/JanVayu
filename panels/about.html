About JanVayu

JanVayu (जनवायु — “People’s Air”) is a non-partisan, citizen-led platform documenting India’s air quality crisis — its live data, its human toll, its policies, and its public memory. It brings together real-time AQI across 115+ cities, a ward-level atlas, a live forecast and farm-fire tracker, health and economic-impact research, policy and accountability tracking, RTI tools, the Ask JanVayu AI assistant, and a multilingual wall of citizen testimony — every figure sourced and open. It is not a campaign; it is a public record, built for the #AQIForJanHit effort.

Our Mission

JanVayu exists because clean air is a fundamental right, not a privilege. We measure the distance between what governments promise and what people actually breathe — using independent data, peer-reviewed research and RTI responses that anyone can check for themselves.

This platform is part of AirQuality for Janhit by MMSF Fellows, AIPC — the broader #AQIForJanHit campaign. We use real-time monitoring data, peer-reviewed research, RTI responses, and independent analysis to provide the accountability that India's air quality crisis demands.

Principles

Scientific Accuracy

PM2.5 primary reporting over AQI. WHO 2021 guideline alignment. GEMM model for health estimates. All claims sourced and verified.

Environmental Justice

Air pollution disproportionately affects marginalized communities. Our analysis centers the burden on informal workers, women, Dalit communities, and the urban poor.

Independent Verification

Government self-reporting is insufficient. We correlate promises with measurable outcomes using RTI, satellite data, and academic research.

Collaborate With Us

JanVayu is built by a small founding team and welcomes collaborators across five workstreams: Research & Data, Policy Analysis, Writing & Content, Technical, and Community & Outreach. You contribute under your own name with full attribution. No minimum commitment — do what you can, when you can.

Research

Add papers to the Zotero library. Translate findings into platform-legible numbers. Fill city and state data gaps.

Policy

Track NCAP budgets, court orders, and government promises vs reality. Write policy briefs for the blog.

Technical

Pull requests on GitHub. Mobile performance, WCAG accessibility, city expansion, AI features.
