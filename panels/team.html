The people behind JanVayu

JanVayu is a non-partisan, citizen-led project — built and maintained by a small group of volunteers who believe clean air is a right, not a privilege. It runs on public data, open source, and people who care. Here’s who’s behind it.

Varna Founder & Lead

Started JanVayu to turn India’s scattered air-quality data into one public record any citizen, journalist, or policymaker can actually use — and to keep it honest, sourced, and free.
