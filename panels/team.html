The people behind JanVayu

JanVayu is a non-partisan, citizen-led project — built and maintained by a small group of volunteers who believe clean air is a right, not a privilege. It runs on public data, open source, and people who care. Here’s who’s behind it.

Want to help? Here's exactly how.

We're a tiny volunteer team, so specific, concrete help moves JanVayu further than anything. Pick whatever fits — each of these genuinely advances the project:

  • Test Vayu Gauraiya, our chatbot. Ask Ask JanVayu hard or imaginative questions — in any language — like "how do I convince my RWA to stop burning leaves?" If an answer invents a fact, cites a source that doesn't exist, or just feels off, send us the exact question and answer. This directly trains our weekly bot-quality checks.
  • Add your testimony. Tell us, in your language, what the air is like where you live — we add real voices to the Citizen Testimony wall.
  • Suggest a sourced fact for the "Did You Know" wall (include where it's from — we never publish an unsourced number).
  • Translate a panel into Hindi, Tamil, Marathi or Bengali.
  • Report something stale or wrong — a figure that looks off, a broken link, an aged claim, a layout bug on your phone.
  • Write code — pick a good first issue (start with CONTRIBUTING.md).
  • Run our social media. We're deliberately not on social yet; if you'd enjoy running it well, join the team and lead it.
Thank you — got it. A real person on the team will read this and reply. Being part of JanVayu is being part of keeping India's air honest.
