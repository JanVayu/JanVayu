# Platform overview

JanVayu is one web page with about 58 panels, arranged in 7 groups by what you want to do: My Air, Maps & Places, Health & Trends, Accountability, Take Action, Learn and Resources. Together they cover live data, health, policy and legal accountability.

---

## Platform sections

| # | Section | What it shows |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **Real-time AQI dashboard** | Live PM2.5 and AQI for 157 Indian cities, plus Beijing, London and Singapore for comparison, from WAQI and CPCB. 33 cities refresh every 10 minutes and the rest load when you select them. It includes cigarette equivalence (Berkeley Earth: 1 cig ≈ 22 µg/m³·day), disease-risk badges and a card of recommendations for the current AQI band |
| 2 | **"Near Me" geolocation** | One tap finds the nearest station, using your browser's location |
| 3 | **Interactive AQI map** | A map with station-level markers, a heatmap toggle, station popups and a historical time slider showing monthly PM2.5 from Jan 2024 to the present |
| 4 | **City rankings** | Rankings of Indian cities for now, the past 7 days and the past 30 days, which you can sort and search |
| 5 | **My Neighbourhood** | CPCB/WAQI stations combined with free Sensor.Community sensors run by residents, each marked by its source |
| 6 | **Health impact calculator** | A simplified risk score based on the logarithm of the concentration (inspired by the GEMM, but not the GEMM function itself). It gives a relative mortality-risk percentage, life years lost and cigarette equivalence |
| 7 | **Economic cost analysis** | GDP loss, healthcare spending and productivity impact (Lancet 2025, World Bank) |
| 8 | **Children's health** | Effects on lung development, school closures and stunting data |
| 9 | **Indoor air quality** | Household pollution from cooking with solid fuel, and how far the Ujjwala scheme has reached |
| 10 | **City comparison** | A real-time AQI comparison, plus a year-over-year tab (2024/2025/2026 monthly averages) |
| 11 | **Historical trends** | An hourly 24-hour PM2.5 chart you can scrub through, an annual trend line, the seasonal pattern and a timeline of key events |
| 12 | **Policy tracker** | NCAP targets against actual outcomes, and GRAP stage history |
| 13 | **Budget tracker** | How much of the NCAP and 15th Finance Commission funds has been spent |
| 14 | **Political accountability** | MoEFCC spending, state compliance and RTI templates |
| 15 | **Industrial sources** | Factory emissions, thermal power plants and brick kilns |
| 16 | **Clean Air Mission tracker** | Government promises set against what happened |
| 17 | **Clean air wins** | Progress so far: e-bus deployment, city improvements and citizen victories |
| 18 | **Legal framework** | Supreme Court orders, NGT rulings and the Article 21 right to clean air |
| 19 | **Take action** | Advocacy guides, citizen tools and campaign resources |
| 20 | **Workshops** | Request an air quality workshop with Dr. Sarath Guttikunda (UrbanEmissions), including the Air Quality Jeopardy game, or book a 1-hour JanVayu walkthrough |
| 21 | **Reading list** | Curated reports from CREA, CSE, IQAir, Lancet and the World Bank |
| 22 | **Citizen voices archive** | Social media posts, testimonies and viral content from affected communities |
| 23 | **Accountability tracker** | How institutions and officials responded to pollution episodes |
| 24 | **Social media feeds** | Reddit, Instagram and news coverage gathered in one place (X is linked, not fetched) |
| 25 | **Daily email digest** | A daily AQI summary for your city at 8:00 AM IST |
| 26 | **AQI calculator** | A tool for understanding AQI breakpoints and health advice |
| 27 | **RTI templates** | Ready-to-use Right to Information templates for asking about pollution |
| 28 | **Cultural archive** | Satire, memes, art and other cultural responses to the pollution crisis |
| 29 | **Pages for each pollutant** | Separate pages at `/pm25`, `/pm10`, `/co`, `/no2`, `/so2` and `/o3`, with sources, health effects and live readings for the 10 most polluted cities |
| 30 | **Embeddable widgets** | `/embed/aqi/?city=...` (a live AQI badge) and `/embed/rankings/?n=10`, which any other website can display |
| 31 | **Install as an app** | Installs on Android, iOS and desktop, and keeps the basic page and the last-known AQI available offline |
| 32 | **"Did you know" facts strip** | Six India-specific, sourced fact cards on the dashboard under the anomaly banner: PM2.5 mortality, life-expectancy loss, the NCAP fund split, the gap between NAAQS and WHO, dose-response, and the Loni headline |
| 33 | **Learning games** | `/#games` has seven self-paced games: Air Quality Jeopardy (₹1,000–₹5,000 INR tiles, ₹75,000 maximum), a 10-question PM Quick-Quiz, Source Matcher, Clean Air Snakes & Ladders (after *Moksha Patam*), Jodi Match (memory cards), Air Tambola (Indian housie) and Vayu Junction (a connections puzzle). All content is original, set in India, and sourced from Lancet Countdown 2025, IQAir 2025, CEEW 2024 and NCAP records |
| 34 | **Ask JanVayu app** | An installable AI chat at `/ask/`, with a chat-style interface, a 33-city chip selector, thumbs up or down feedback and an offline fallback. It is available in 10 languages |
| 35 | **April–May 2026 voices** | Curated reactions to the Lancet Countdown launch, a ground report from Loni residents, Soumya Swaminathan at the "Be Cool" launch, the Supreme Court's four-week deadline, Warrior Moms commentary and the CSE NCAP review |
| 36 | **April–May 2026 research updates** | A group of featured cards at the top of the Reading List: Lancet Countdown 2025, AQLI 2025, IQAir 2025, the CSE NCAP review, Jaganathan et al. 2024 in Lancet Planetary Health, and CEEW 2024 source apportionment |
| 37 | **Women's health** | Indoor cooking exposure, maternal health, occupational risks, the gender data gap and an evaluation of the Ujjwala scheme |
| 38 | **Understanding AQI** | An interactive breakdown of how the AQI is built from up to eight pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb). The live data and the diagram carry the first six, because ammonia and lead are measured at fewer stations. It also compares the CPCB and US EPA AQI scales and explains why PM2.5 is not the whole story |
| 39 | **Shareable AQI cards** | Makes a PNG card (1080x1080 for Instagram, 1200x630 for WhatsApp), coloured by severity. Share buttons appear on the dashboard, rankings, map and comparisons, and use the phone's share sheet |
| 40 | **Exposure diary** | Log your daily routine (16 activities, each with a calibrated PM2.5 multiplier) to get a weighted daily exposure, cigarette equivalence, life-expectancy impact, a stacked bar chart and personalised tips |
| 41 | **Migration comparison** | Compares two cities side by side: live AQI for both, a 7-row table, source apportionment bar charts and a verdict in life-years gained |
| 42 | **Data source selector** | Explains how CPCB, WAQI, IQAir and Sensor.Community differ, with toggle switches, details of instruments, accuracy and coverage, and a Source Impact Simulator |
| 43 | **City policy tracker** | An 8-city NCAP target dashboard with expenditure tables, a timeline of government action, a public feedback section and governance questions |
