- **[remove]**  — 15th Finance Commission grants (₹16,539 Cr) expired the same day (31 March 2026)

- **[fix]**  — Indoor plants — Money plant, snake plant, areca palm (₹100-300 each). NASA study: 3-4 plants per room helps.

The 2019 Drexel meta-review (Cummings & Waring, J. NASA never recommended '3-4 plants per room' for air cleaning. Surgically removing the false sourcing while keeping the plant listing itself.  
  *Source:* Cummings & Waring, J. Exposure Sci. Environ. Epidemiol. 2019; NASA/Wolverton 1989 (context)

- **[fix]**  — The AQI is the worst of SIX separate sub-indices — one each for PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, and O3.  
  The CPCB National AQI is calculated from EIGHT pollutants, not six. Its official methodology adds NH3 (ammonia) and Pb (lead) to the six listed. Confirmed via CPCB 'About National Air Quality Index' document: sub-indices and health breakpoints are defined for PM10, PM2.5, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, and Pb. The composite AQI is the maximum of these eight sub-indices (min. 3 pollutants incl. one PM). This is a hard methodological fact, not an accountability narrative, so a straight correction is safe.  
  *Source:* CPCB, About National Air Quality Index (2014); cpcb.nic.in

Adding the age-group scope preserves the number and citation while removing the overgeneralisation.  
  *Source:* Analitis A. et al. (2014), Epidemiology 25(1):15-22, PubMed 24162013
