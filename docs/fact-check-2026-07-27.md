## Corrected (applied, with source)

### `panels/resources.html` — the recurrence of the false "70%" claim (CRITICAL)

- Two resource cards still said India = "~70% of global burden" / "India = 70% of Global Deaths" (one in the card *title*, with "70 percent" also baked into the `data-keywords` search index). This is precisely the false figure `stats.json` and `index.html` were corrected to fix weeks ago — it just never got fixed here.
  `India = ~70% of global burden` → `roughly a quarter to a third of the global ambient PM2.5 burden, NOT a majority`
  `Lancet Countdown: India = 70% of Global Deaths` (title) → `Lancet Countdown 2025: India's PM2.5 Death Toll`
  *Source:* Lancet Countdown 2025 India Data Sheet (30 Oct 2025); cross-checked global-share math this round (~30%, "nearly one-third" per multiple secondary reports) against last round's ~25–38% range — both converge in the high-20s/low-30s%, so the site now says "a quarter to a third" rather than a single point estimate neither round could pin down precisely.

### State of Global Air total-death figure — superseded edition, site-wide

- **State of Global Air 2025 (HEI/IHME, GBD 2023 data, published 23 Oct 2025) supersedes the 2024 edition (GBD 2021 data)** the site had been citing for its "total incl. household air pollution" companion figure to the 1.72M ambient-PM2.5 number. SoGA 2025 reports India's total air-pollution death toll at **2.0 million (2023 data)**, up from 1.4 million in 2000 (43% increase) — and separately, dementia was added as an attributable outcome for the first time (this new-edition detail was already correctly present in a `resources.html` card, which is what surfaced the discrepancy).
  `~2.1 million (State of Global Air 2024, GBD 2021)` → `~2.0 million (State of Global Air 2025, 2023 data)`
  Fixed in `index.html` (Did You Know card), `panels/faq.html`, `panels/economic.html` (stat card), `panels/budget.html`, and `netlify/functions/air-query.mjs`. Per standing policy, **both** death-toll figures are kept — 1.72M ambient-PM2.5 (Lancet Countdown 2025) and ~2.0M total-incl.-household (State of Global Air 2025) — each labelled with its own scope and year; neither collapses into the other.
  *Note:* `blog/posts/2026-07-17-we-factchecked-ourselves.md` still illustrates this same principle using the older "~2.1 million (State of Global Air 2024)" figure. Left unchanged — per the dated-record rule established in the 17 Jul round, a historical blog post recording what was true/decided at time of writing is not retroactively edited; the live claim locations (index.html, faq.html, the chatbot prompt) are what matter and are now current.
  *Source:* Health Effects Institute / IHME, "State of Global Air 2025," stateofglobalair.org, published 23 October 2025.

- The panel's own prose paragraph already correctly dates and attributes both the $36.8B (GBD 2019/Lancet Planetary Health) and $339B (Lancet Countdown 2025) figures — that pairing was reviewed and confirmed defensible in the 17 Jul round and is unchanged.   *Source:* Lancet Countdown 2025 India Data Sheet (2022 data); State of Global Air 2025 (2023 data).

### `panels/budget.html` — "Gap: Budget vs. Burden" card had a live arithmetic inconsistency

- The card's own "spending = 0.5% of economic burden" note is computed as ~₹15,000 Cr (~$1.8B) ÷ $339B = 0.53% → "0.5%" — **but the headline economic-cost figure directly above it said $36.8B**, against which the same spending would be 4.9%, not 0.5%. The card's own math was silently using a different denominator than its own displayed headline. Promoted $339.4 billion (9.5% GDP, Lancet Countdown 2025) to the headline — now consistent with its own ratio calculation — and moved the older GBD 2019 estimate ($36.8B / 1.67M deaths / 17.8% / the $28.8B+$8.0B mortality/morbidity split) into a clearly labelled secondary line, explicitly noting it uses a different method (output/welfare-loss vs. VSL-monetised mortality) and is kept for reference rather than summed with the current figure. Updated the death-toll bullet to the current 1.72M ambient / ~2.0M total pairing and the source footer to lead with Lancet Countdown 2025 + State of Global Air 2025.
