India's air is killing 1.72 million people every year

JanVayu is India's independent, citizen-led air quality platform. We track live pollution data, measure health impacts, follow the money, and hold governments accountable. Because clean air is not a privilege, it is a right.

July 2026: The monsoon brings a brief respite, but India's air remains in crisis year-round. The most recent IQAir World Air Quality Report (the 2025 edition, published March 2026 covering 2025 data) ranks Loni, India as the most polluted city on Earth (112.5 µg/m³ — 22× the WHO guideline); India's average PM2.5 of 48.9 µg/m³ is nearly 10× the WHO limit; only 14% of global cities meet safe air standards. The Lancet Countdown 2025 (published October 2025) attributes 1.72 million Indian deaths per year to ambient PM2.5 — among the world’s largest national tolls. NCAP's 31 March 2026 deadline has elapsed: only 23 of 96 cities with sufficient data met the 40% PM10 reduction target (CREA, Tracing the Hazy Air 2026). CAQM invoked Stage-I GRAP off-season for the first time on 19 May 2026, then revoked it on 29 May 2026 as the monsoon set in — the calm months mask a year-round crisis. New: a seven-game Learning Games panel is now live — including the new Vayu Junction word-grouping puzzle — and a native guided walkthrough of the whole platform, now a self-hosted HTML deck we keep in sync with the site.

The evidence

Did you know

Six sourced India-specific facts · the most recent published values from each source (Lancet Countdown 2025, IQAir 2025, AQLI 2025, CSE 2026, Jaganathan et al. 2024).

1.72M
Indian PM2.5 deaths a year
Ambient PM2.5, Lancet Countdown 2025 (2022 data) — up from 1.5M last cycle, and among the world’s largest national tolls (roughly a quarter to a third of the global PM2.5 burden). Counting household air pollution too, State of Global Air 2025 puts India at ~2.0M (2023 data).
112.5 µg/m³
Loni, India — the world’s most polluted city
IQAir World Air Quality Report 2025 (covering 2025 data, published March 2026; the most recent annual). Loni in Ghaziabad district, UP, dethroned Byrnihat. The annual average is 22× the WHO guideline.
3.5 yrs
Life expectancy lost on average
AQLI 2025 (UChicago EPIC). Delhi residents lose up to 8.2 years — the largest loss of any major city; the Indo-Gangetic Plain remains the world’s most polluted region.
64%
of NCAP funds spent on dust suppression
CSE 2026 NCAP review. Mostly mechanical sweeping & water-spraying — not the combustion sources that drive most PM2.5.
8×
India’s NAAQS vs WHO guideline
India NAAQS = 40 µg/m³ annual PM2.5; WHO 2021 guideline = 5. Indian standard last revised in 2009.
8.6%
Mortality rise per +10 µg/m³ PM2.5
Jaganathan et al. 2024, Lancet Planetary Health. India’s first nationwide causal estimate — a difference-in-differences design across 655 districts.
