    September 2026

    The monsoon withdraws from northwest India around the middle of this month, and with it goes the stretch when much of the country breathes its cleanest air of the year. October opens the season everyone argues about: paddy stubble burning across Punjab and Haryana, Diwali, and the temperature inversions that hold everything down. What follows is not weather. It is the same set of decisions, taken again.The rains end in the middle of this month. That ends the cleanest few weeks of the year for most of the country. Then come crop fires in Punjab and Haryana, Diwali, and cold air that traps the smoke near the ground. This is not bad luck. The same choices are made every year.

    Where the numbers come from

    Live readings come from India's continuous CPCB monitoring network, which is why they exist only where a machine does. Annual satellite estimates close the gap and reach every ward, panchayat and village. Eleven years of CPCB's own daily bulletins sit behind both.Live numbers come from government machines that measure the air. There is a machine only in some places. For everywhere else we use satellite pictures, which give a figure for the whole year rather than for today.
