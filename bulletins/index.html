Every city in CPCB’s daily bulletin

Each day at 4 pm the Central Pollution Control Board publishes one air quality figure for each of about 290 cities. This page shows every one of those figures for 2026, city by city, to 18 November 2026.

Updated from CPCB’s bulletin each day. The figures run to 18 November 2026 for 319 cities.

How to read it

The figure for a day is CPCB’s city index for the 24 hours to 4 pm, from the stations that reported. The station count beside it says how many reported out of how many the city has. A figure from one station describes one place with a city’s name on it, and a day when half the stations were silent cannot be set beside a full day without saying so.

The index reports whichever pollutant scores worst on the day, so the page counts days in each category and gives no average. The comparison counts the same calendar days in 2019 to 2025. Earlier years are left out because the number of stations behind most cities changed too much to compare. A different count of days reported in a year is shown, not filled in.

Comparing one window with the same window in another year shows what the bulletin recorded. It does not say why one year differed from another: weather alone moves these counts a long way.

Sources

  • Central Pollution Control Board, National Air Quality Index daily bulletin, published as a PDF each day at 4 pm. JanVayu parses every 2026 bulletin; the table is data/raw/cpcb-bulletins-2026.csv.gz.
  • For 2019 to 2025, UrbanEmissions.Info’s extraction of the same PDFs, github.com/urbanemissionsinfo/AQI_bulletins, which also has its own explorer and SQL playground. This page publishes only the counts made from it. Comparing that extraction with JanVayu’s for the 297 days of 2026 both hold found one date where JanVayu had filed the wrong day’s bulletin, now corrected.
  • Full-year counts for every city since 2015 are in data/aqi-bulletins.json.
